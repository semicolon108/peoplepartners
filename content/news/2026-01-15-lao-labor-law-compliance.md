Navigating the Lao Labor Law can be complex for international entities entering the market. While the law is comprehensive, enforcement and interpretation can vary.

## Top 3 Common Compliance Mistakes

### 1. Overtime (OT) Calculation
Many companies incorrectly calculate OT based on the **Net Salary** instead of the **Basic Salary**. The law explicitly states that OT rates (150%, 200%, 250%) must be applied to the hourly breakdown of the basic wage.

### 2. Termination without Cause
Differeing from some western jurisdictions, "At-will employment" does not exist in Laos. Terminating an employee without valid grounds (as defined in Article 80) requires significant severance pay, regardless of notice period.

### 3. Foreign Quotas
Remember that the ratio of foreign to local employees is strictly regulated.
*   **Physical Labor**: 15% foreigners max.
*   **Intellectual/Specialized**: 25% foreigners max.

## Solutions

To ensure you are fully compliant:
*   Review your Internal Regulations.
*   Audit your employment contracts.
*   Consult with local experts.
